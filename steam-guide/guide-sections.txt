==================== SECTION: Overview ====================
[h1]Interactive maps for every mission[/h1]
Every campaign mission, hub, challenge, skirmish and co-op map, with everything that is placed on it when the level starts: nests, foraging spots, resources, golden beetles, butterflies, lost sisters, quest givers, scans, checkpoints and creatures.

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Pick a map from the index (or from the lists in this guide).
[*]Click a marker for its in-game icon, screenshot, bestiary or collection text, quest dialogue and details (nest owner, food or wood, tier, guardian).
[*]Use the categories on the left to show or hide markers ("Hide all", then switch on only what you are hunting for).
[*]Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. Works in any browser, including the Steam overlay browser (Shift+Tab).
[/list]

[spoiler]The maps show every collectible's position, so treat them as spoilers if you want to find things yourself.[/spoiler]

[list]
[*]Positions are where things are placed or spawned when the level starts. Scripted spawns and patrol routes are not shown.
[*]Maps with an in-game minimap use it as the background. Levels without one (hubs, exploration, traversal and cave missions) show a terrain relief made from the level's own geometry (light = high, dark = low) with a grid; toggle them at the top right.
[*]"Fortification" and "Intrusion" (Chapter 1 tutorials) have approximate marker positions.
[*]Challenge-mode and skirmish maps share layouts with their campaign versions but are listed separately because their setups differ.
[/list]
